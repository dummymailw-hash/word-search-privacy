Privacy Policy

Word Search Voyage · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary

Word Search Voyage is an offline word puzzle game. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game has no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

To remember your game, the app saves the following only on your own phone:

  • Levels completed and stars earned
  • Coins, daily puzzle results and streak
  • Settings such as sound, vibration and dark mode

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Reset progress in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet and permissions

The game works fully offline and makes no network requests. The Android internet permission is included only because the system component that displays the game requires it. The app may use vibration for touch feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Third-party services

The app does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social media services.

Children's privacy

Word Search Voyage is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
